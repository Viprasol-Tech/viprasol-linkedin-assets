# Brand Brief — Viprasol Tech

> Captured: 2026-06-29
> Update this file as the business evolves.

## Business

Viprasol Tech turns peer-reviewed quantitative trading research into live, risk-gated trading algorithms — for individual traders, not hedge funds. Two flavors:

1. **Golden Viper EA** ($99/mo SaaS at goldenviperea.com) — managed signal/algo subscription.
2. **Custom builds** — clients bring an edge (or pick one from our 440-strategy research library), we engineer it into production with execution, position sizing, and risk gates.

The honest core: we don't sell promises of returns. We share verified strategy logic openly and earn business through demonstrated competence.

## Customer

A self-directed retail trader, age ~30-55, mostly male, technical mindset. Probably trades stocks, futures, FX, or crypto. Has read about momentum, value, carry, vol-targeting, but doesn't have the coding or systems chops to backtest and deploy them properly. Currently either:
- Hand-trading discretionary setups and burning out
- Using off-the-shelf indicators / EAs from sketchy vendors who promise 200% returns
- Aspiring to "build my own algo" but stuck in Python tutorials

They want **someone competent to either give them the strategy logic OR build the system for them** — without being marketed at with screenshots of fake equity curves.

Where they hang out: LinkedIn (algorithmic trading communities), Twitter/X (#quant, #algotrading), Reddit (r/algotrading), YouTube (quant content channels).

## Primary CTA

**DM a keyword** (one per post, e.g. "TREND", "WINNERS") for the complete algo + research file. Soft-secondary: explore custom builds. Long-term funnel: convert to Golden Viper EA subscription.

## Voice

**Expert and authoritative · raw and honest · scrappy and underdog.**

5-word self-description: **mechanical, peer-reviewed, no fluff, anti-hype.**

Voice signature:
- Specific numbers and journal citations (JF 2012, RFS 2014).
- Strategy names in CAPS for emphasis (TIME-SERIES MOMENTUM, CARRY).
- Bullet lists with 🔹 prefix.
- One soft CTA per post (DM a keyword).
- Emojis used sparingly for visual scan-anchors (📊 🎯 🚀 👇) — never as decoration.
- Always ends with the disclaimer that we share strategy, sell systems.

## Universal Voice Rules (apply to every post)

- Contractions always ("don't" not "do not")
- Active voice, short sentences
- Address the reader as "you"
- Numbers as digits ("3 tips" not "three tips")
- **No em dashes** — use commas, periods, or "..."
- One concrete strategy per post, not three
- Specific journal/year for every claim
- **NEVER claim a return number.** Not "100% backtested," not "Sharpe 2.5 on my account." The strategy is described; performance is the reader's to verify. The single non-negotiable rule.
- Disclaimer block on long-form posts: "Strategy shared free. Performance depends on execution, sizing, and risk gates — that's the part we engineer."

## What we DON'T do (anti-patterns)

- No fake equity curves, no broker screenshots, no "I made $X this month" hooks.
- No comment-bait ("type YES to receive the algo"). The DM keyword is a soft funnel, not engagement bait.
- No urgency ("limited slots, act now") unless genuinely true.
- No "AI generated my strategy" — every strategy is from peer-reviewed research, AI is only used in the image pipeline.
- No vague aspiration content ("trade like the pros") — every post must teach one specific verified mechanism.
